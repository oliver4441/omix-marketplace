5 Signs Your Business Needs a Website
(Even If You Have Google Maps)

A practical guide for Kenyan business owners

1

Customers Find You but Leave Without Contacting You

Your Google Maps listing shows your business, but potential customers want more information before reaching out. Without a website, they have nowhere to learn about your services, pricing, or why they should choose you. A website gives them the confidence to take the next step.

2

You're Losing Customers to Competitors Who Have Websites

When customers compare businesses on Google Maps, the ones with professional websites stand out. They appear more established, trustworthy, and serious about their business. Without a website, you're sending customers to your competitors every single day.

3

You're Answering the Same Questions Over and Over

"What are your hours?" "How much do you charge?" "Do you offer this service?" A website answers these questions automatically, 24 hours a day. This saves you time and ensures customers have the information they need before they call or visit.

4

You Can't Accept Payments or Bookings Online

Google Maps doesn't let customers pay for your services or book appointments. A website with M-Pesa integration and booking systems means customers can pay and book any time, even while you sleep. This is how modern businesses in Kenya are growing.

5

You Have No Control Over How Your Business Appears Online

On Google Maps, your listing looks the same as everyone else's. You can't tell your story, showcase your work, or highlight what makes you different. A website gives you full control over your brand message and customer experience.
